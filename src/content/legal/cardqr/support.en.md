# CardQR Support

For help with CardQR, email **support@0xvera.com**.

## Please include

- App version
- Android version
- Device model
- A clear description of the problem

## Do not send sensitive data

Do not send QR codes, documents, personal identifiers or other sensitive material unless it is strictly necessary to reproduce an issue—and even then, redact what you can.

There is no ticketing portal yet. Email is the supported channel for this phase.
