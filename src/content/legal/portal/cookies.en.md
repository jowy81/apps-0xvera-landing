# Cookie Notice

**Last updated:** 2026-08-06

This notice explains how `apps.0xvera.com` uses cookies and similar technologies.

## Summary

- No analytics cookies are set before you accept analytics.
- Rejecting analytics is as easy as accepting.
- You can change your choice later using “Manage analytics” in the footer.
- The site works fully without accepting analytics.

## Essential / functional storage

| Name / key | Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `oxvera-analytics-consent` | `localStorage` | Stores whether you accepted or rejected analytics |
| `oxvera-theme` | `localStorage` | Stores light, dark or system theme preference |

These values are stored in your browser and are not used as advertising identifiers.

## Analytics (optional)

If you accept analytics **and** a GA4 measurement ID is configured, Google Analytics 4 may set cookies or use similar storage controlled by Google. Typical GA4 cookies can include identifiers used to distinguish sessions and measure aggregate traffic.

GA4 is not loaded until consent is granted.

If you reject analytics, this site will not load GA4 for that browser profile.

## Managing your choice

Use the “Manage analytics” control in the site footer, or clear site data in your browser settings.
